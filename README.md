## 🔹 Looker Studio Dashboard

### Product Sales Dashboard
**Tool:** Looker Studio  
**Description:**  
An interactive dashboard designed to track product sales performance across multiple product categories and time periods. The dashboard highlights key metrics such as total quantity sold, revenue, and product line performance, enabling quick identification of top-performing and underperforming categories.

**Key Features:**
- KPI cards for Quantity Sold and Revenue
- Trend analysis by product line
- Interactive filters for exploration
- Clean, stakeholder-friendly layout

🔗 **View Dashboard:** https://lookerstudio.google.com/s/j7Dhn6rf_ic
